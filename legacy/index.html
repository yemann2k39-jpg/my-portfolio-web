About Me

Building Solutions for Real Friction

I am a software developer who thrives at the intersection of design, logic, and human behavior. I don't just write code. I identify friction points in daily digital life and engineer applied solutions to fix them.

Whether it's unifying fragmented study workflows or cutting through internet hype to surface authentic experiences, my goal is to build technology that serves the user honestly and efficiently.

My Approach

Building great software requires more than just functional code. It requires understanding how people think, interact, and solve problems in real-world environments. I value creating systems that balance technical reliability with intuitive and thoughtful user experiences.

I am passionate about building technology that is not only technically sound, but also meaningful, practical, and genuinely valuable to the people who use it.
